How to play

Your hand is your weapon.

If you know poker, you already know how to fight. If you don't, Old Bram teaches you in a minute.

  1. Draw your cards

    Eight cards from Bram's magic deck land in your hand.

  2. Build the best hand

    Pick up to five cards. Discard the rest and draw again if you need a better hand.

  3. Attack!

    Bigger hands hit harder. A royal flush shakes the whole screen.

  4. Survive the counter

    Every monster hits back. Armor, potions and spells keep you standing.
