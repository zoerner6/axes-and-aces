Axes & Aces Privacy Policy

Axes & Aces does not collect, store or share any personal information.

Your saved game is kept only on your own device, in the app's local storage. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/zoerner6/axes-and-aces. Website: axesandaces.com.
