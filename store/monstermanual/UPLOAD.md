# Putting the Monster Manual on sale — step by step

Nothing has been uploaded. Every step below is yours to do.

There are two editions, built from the same source:

| Edition | Where | Price | File |
|---|---|---|---|
| PDF | itch.io | $14.99 | `dist/Axes-and-Aces-Monster-Manual.pdf` |
| Paperback | Amazon KDP | $29.99 | `dist/Axes-and-Aces-Monster-Manual-KDP-interior.pdf` + `-KDP-cover.pdf` |

Do the itch.io one first. It takes fifteen minutes and needs no decisions.
The paperback is worth more money but has one choice in it you cannot undo.

---

## Stores that are closed to this book

So nobody re-treads this ground.

**DriveThruRPG, Roll20 and DMs Guild.** One policy covers all three. Their
Product Standards Guidelines say, in their own words:

> Your content must be conceived and written by a human. We do not accept any
> product that includes text generated by AI tools.

Their banned-tools list names Claude and ChatGPT directly. Their AI *art* rule
would have been survivable — they explicitly allow AI art inside a rulebook,
which is why this was built as a book — but the text rule is absolute and
editing the words does not change where they came from. Checked 1 November
2026 against the live page.

**Foundry VTT's package browser.** It stopped listing packages containing
AI art or AI text in March 2026. The free module still works, it just installs
from a link instead of the in-app browser.

**Kindle.** Not a store rule, a judgement: this is a 138-page full-colour
8.5x11 book of stat blocks. Reflowable Kindle would destroy the layout and
fixed-layout Kindle reads badly on a phone. The PDF on itch.io already serves
digital buyers. Skip it.

## Part one: the PDF on itch.io (about 15 minutes)

1. **Sign in to itch.io in Chrome.** If you do not have an account, make one
   at `itch.io/register`. Use your normal Chrome so the password is saved.

2. Go to **`itch.io/game/new`**.

3. **Title:** `Axes & Aces Monster Manual`

4. **Short description:** copy the tagline from `STORE.md`.

5. **Classification:** **Physical game**. That is itch's name for tabletop
   things; it does not mean you are shipping anyone a box.

6. **AI generation disclosure:** set it to **yes, generated with AI**. Do not
   skip this. Untagged AI projects get pulled out of browse, and the tag is
   the condition itch sets for allowing the book at all.

7. **Pricing:** **Paid**, **$14.99**.

8. **Uploads:** upload `Axes-and-Aces-Monster-Manual.pdf`. It is about 95 MB,
   so give it a minute. Tick **This file will be sold**.

9. **Description:** paste the long description from `STORE.md`.

10. **Cover image:** upload `dist/cover-itch.png`.

11. **Tags:** paste the tag list from `STORE.md`. Note it no longer has
    "dnd" or "dungeons-and-dragons" in it, on purpose — `STORE.md` says why.

12. **Save**, then **View page**, read it over, then set **Visibility** to
    **Public** and Save again.

13. **Launch sale:** Edit → Distribute → Sale, **25% off for one week**.

## Part two: the paperback on Amazon KDP

Bigger audience and better money per copy, but read step 4 before you start.

**The files are already print-ready.** The interior is laid out on a
8.625 x 11.25 inch sheet, which is the 8.5 x 11 trim plus Amazon's 0.125 inch
bleed on the top, bottom and outside edge, and the trim box alternates sides
so the gutter lands correctly. The cover is a single 17.561 x 11.25 inch PDF
holding back, spine and front, with the spine sized for 138 pages.

1. **Sign in to KDP** at `kdp.amazon.com`. Same account the coloring book is
   going through.

2. **Create → Paperback.**

3. **Language** English. **Title:** `Axes & Aces Monster Manual`.
   **Subtitle:** `168 Monsters in Ten Worlds, Fifth Edition Compatible`.
   Keep "Dungeons & Dragons" out of the title, subtitle, description and
   keywords — `STORE.md` explains why.

4. **This is the question to get right: AI content.** Somewhere in the
   Content step KDP asks you to declare AI-generated content. **Answer yes,
   for both text and images.** Their rule, in their words:

   > We require you to inform us of AI-generated content (text, images, or
   > translations) when you publish a new book… If you used an AI-based tool
   > to create the actual content… it is considered "AI-generated," even if
   > you applied substantial edits afterwards.

   The pictures were made by an image model and the words were written by a
   language model, so both are AI-generated, not AI-assisted. Declaring it is
   what makes the book allowed. It is the same answer the coloring book gives,
   which matters because both books come from the same art.

   **This is what the form actually says**, read off the live page on
   2 November 2026. It sits near the bottom of the Content step, under a
   heading "AI-Generated Content":

   > Did you use AI tools in creating texts, images, and/or translations in
   > your book?

   Answer **Yes**. Three dropdowns then appear — Texts, Images and
   Translations — each offering the same five choices: None; Some sections,
   with minimal or no editing; Some sections, with extensive editing; Entire
   work, with minimal or no editing; Entire work, with extensive editing.

   Set them to:

   | | |
   |---|---|
   | Texts | Entire work, with minimal or no editing |
   | Images | Entire work, with minimal or no editing |
   | Translations | None |

   "Editing" here means editing **you** did by hand after the AI made it, and
   you did none — the revisions this book went through were made by the same
   AI that wrote it. Amazon's own rule is that content stays AI-generated
   "even if you applied substantial edits afterwards", so the editing answer
   is informational, not a way out. Saying "minimal or no editing" discloses
   more rather than less, which is the safe direction on a declaration.

   One thing Amazon still does not say: whether this declaration is shown to
   customers. I previously wrote here that it stays private, and I should not
   have, because their pages do not say so. What their pages do say is that
   there is no AI-specific penalty attached to answering yes.

5. **ISBN:** take the free KDP one unless you want to own the ISBN.

6. **Print options:**
   - Ink: **Standard colour**
   - Paper: **White**
   - Trim: **8.5 x 11 in** (click "Select a different size")
   - Bleed: **Bleed (PDF only)**
   - Cover finish: **Matte**

   **Ink type cannot be changed once the book is published.** Amazon locks it
   to the ISBN. Standard colour costs $6.55 a copy to print; premium colour
   costs $12.04 and would force the price above $21 before you earn anything.
   Standard is the right call for a book that is mostly light parchment
   pages, but see step 9 before you commit.

   **If the proof makes you switch to premium, the cover has to be rebuilt.**
   The spine is thicker on premium paper — 0.324 in against 0.311 in — and a
   cover built for the wrong one will have the spine type drifting onto the
   front. Rebuild it from `store/monstermanual/` with:

   ```bash
   python src/book.py --premium
   ```

7. **Upload the interior:** `Axes-and-Aces-Monster-Manual-KDP-interior.pdf`.
   It is 95 MB; KDP's ceiling is 650 MB, so it is well inside.

8. **Upload the cover:** choose "Use a cover you already have" and upload
   `Axes-and-Aces-Monster-Manual-KDP-cover.pdf`.

9. **Run the Previewer, then order a proof.** The previewer catches margin
   and bleed errors. The proof catches the thing the previewer cannot: this
   book has ten full-page dark scenes, and dark full-bleed pages are the
   hardest thing for print-on-demand to do well. If they come back muddy or
   banded, go back to step 6 and switch to premium colour *before* you
   publish, because after publishing you cannot.

10. **Pricing:** list at **$29.99** on Amazon.com. That earns about **$11.45 a
    copy** after printing. The floor Amazon will allow is $10.91; do not go
    near it, print RPG books are not priced like novels.

11. **Expanded Distribution: tick it, but do not count on it.** 8.5 x 11 on
    white paper is eligible on both standard and premium colour, so the chart
    lets you in. The content rules then exclude, in their words, "Paperbacks
    with interior pages that require a high concentration of ink to be
    printed" — which a full-colour bestiary plainly is. It costs nothing to
    try: Amazon says that being refused or removed from Expanded Distribution
    has "no impact into your KDP account status or the availability of your
    book(s) on Amazon."

12. **Publish.** It takes up to 72 hours to go live.

**One scheduling note.** KDP allows **2 new titles per format per week**, for
creation and for submission separately, resetting Sunday 00:00 UTC. The
coloring book is also a paperback, so the two of them use both slots in a
week. Two is enough for both; just do not plan a third paperback in the same
week.

## Part three: the free Foundry VTT module (optional)

Marketing, not a product. It puts the monsters in front of people who buy
bestiaries.

It cannot go in Foundry's own module browser — see the closed-stores list
above — so it goes out as a direct download instead.

0. **Run the build first** so the module's pictures are in place, from
   `store/monstermanual/`:

   ```bash
   python src/make.py
   ```

   The 168 images are copied in from the game rather than kept in the repo
   twice, so without this the folder has everything except the art.

1. Zip `foundry/axes-and-aces-monsters/`.
2. On the itch page, Edit → Uploads, upload the zip, tick **This file is
   free**, name it `Foundry VTT module (free)`.

**It has never been loaded into a real Foundry.** Install it once yourself and
click the import button before telling anyone it works.

## After both are up

1. **Link them to each other.** The itch page should say a paperback exists
   and the Amazon description should say a PDF exists.
2. **Put a link on the game.** A line on AxesAndAces.com is the cheapest sale
   you will ever make — those people already like the monsters.
3. **Do not bother with Gumroad, Payhip or Ko-fi yet.** They all allow the
   book, but none of them brings any buyers of its own. They are checkout
   pages, worth doing only if itch sells and you want a better margin on
   repeat buyers.
