The free Foundry VTT companion to the **Axes & Aces Monster Manual**: all 168 creatures as dnd5e NPCs with their artwork.

**Install from this manifest URL:**

```
https://github.com/zoerner6/axes-and-aces/releases/latest/download/module.json
```

In Foundry: Add-on Modules -> Install Module -> paste that into the Manifest URL box. Foundry's package browser does not list modules containing AI-generated art, so a manifest link is the way in.

Then enable it, open the **Actors** tab and click **Import Axes & Aces monsters**. It builds a world compendium. Once only.

Each creature carries its ability scores, armor class, hit points and hit dice, speeds, senses, languages, challenge rating and XP, resistances, immunities and vulnerabilities, skill and saving throw proficiencies, traits, its attack and its recharging heavy blow. The forty bosses carry legendary actions, the ten that end a world carry lair actions, and the nine spellcasters carry their spell lists.

Needs Foundry v11+ and the dnd5e system v3.0+.

**Not yet tested in a live Foundry.** Every actor validates against the dnd5e schema and the script parses, but nobody has installed it and clicked the button. If something breaks, please open an issue.

**Contains AI-generated content.** Every picture was made with an AI image model and the text was written with AI assistance.
