## Where this can be sold

**DriveThruRPG and Roll20 are closed to this book.** Their Product Standards
page (checked 1 November 2026, last updated 15 September 2026) says two things
that between them rule it out:

> We do not accept any standalone artwork products or content, including comic
> books, graphic novels, art books, or music / audio, that are generated in
> whole or in part by AI.

> Your content must be conceived and written by a human. We do not accept any
> product that includes text generated by AI tools.

The first rule was the one we expected, and the book format gets around it:
they explicitly allow AI art *inside* a rulebook or adventure. The second rule
is the one that kills it. The text in this book was written with AI help, and
their banned-tools list names Claude and ChatGPT directly. One policy covers
DriveThruRPG, DMs Guild and Roll20, so all three are out.

**itch.io is open and is where this should go.** It permits AI art and AI text
in paid products, and asks only that the project is tagged honestly with its
AI Disclosure field. That tag is set in step 6 of the upload checklist.

Backups, if itch ever changes its mind: Gumroad and Payhip have no AI rules at
all, and Ko-fi explicitly permits AI work as long as you do not pass it off as
hand-made. None of those three bring any buyers of their own, so they are
checkout pages rather than stores.

## Price

**Launch at $14.99.**

Comparable bestiaries, checked 1 November 2026:

| Book | Monsters | Price |
|---|---|---|
| All the Kobolds (Pesto Publications) | 30 | $6.95 |
| The Big Book of Big Bads (Hit Point Press) | 61 | $29.99 |
| Creatures & Curios (Hit Point Press) | 100+ | $29.99 |
| The Bastard's Book of Monsters (solo) | ~109 | $25.00 |
| Tome of Beasts 1 Revised (Kobold Press) | 400+ | $39.99 |

Solo publishers get about 12 to 25 cents a monster, which puts 168 monsters at
$17 to $25. But nothing from an unknown publisher in that list clears $25, and
everything above it is an established name with commissioned art. AI art is a
visible price objection on top of being unknown. $14.99 is the honest spot.
$19.99 is the ceiling if you want to try it; do not go above that.

Run **25% off for launch week**. That is normal on itch and buyers there
expect it.

### 168 monsters. Ten worlds. Every one of them ready to drop on a table.

This is the bestiary for **Axes & Aces**, the free dark comic card game where
you fight monsters with poker hands. Every creature in the game is in here,
with a picture, a stat block, and a line telling you how it actually fights.

**What you get**

- **168 monsters**, from a goblin in a tavern to the Demon King on his throne.
- **Full 5E-compatible stat blocks.** Armor class, hit points, speeds, ability
  scores, resistances, traits, actions, challenge rating. Nothing left for you
  to fill in.
- **Forty bosses**, each on a page of its own, each with the one trick that
  makes it different.
- **Ten worlds** to pull from: a tavern cellar, frozen peaks, burning sands, a
  sunken coast, a plague marsh, iron mines, a haunted abbey, elven wilds, a sky
  fortress, and the Abyss.
- **Challenge 1/2 up to 22**, laid out so the book runs easy to hard front to
  back. World one is for a 1st-level party. World ten is for a 20th.
- **A picture for every single monster**, each one standing in its own place
  rather than on a blank page.
- **The method, written down.** The introduction explains exactly how the card
  game's balance numbers became challenge ratings, so you can see the working
  and decide whether to trust it.

**Use it with whatever you are already running.** There is no setting to buy
into and no new rules to learn. It is a book of monsters.

**146 pages, PDF.**

---

**Contains AI-generated content.** Every illustration was generated with an AI
image model, and the text was written with AI assistance. Nothing here was
drawn or written by a human by hand. The pictures come out of the card game,
where they have been in use since launch. If AI art or AI writing is not
something you want to pay for, please do not buy this — that is a fair
position and this notice is here so you can take it before you spend anything.
