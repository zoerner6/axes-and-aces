FULL DESCRIPTION
Axes & Aces is a dark comic card adventure where your poker hands are your weapons.

You are a young soldier at the Gilded Ace tavern. When Anna is taken, you set out to bring her home. Every fight is a hand of cards: discard what you don't need, build the best poker hand you can, and strike. A pair is a jab. A full house is a hammer blow. Aces hit hardest of all.

- 10 quests across frozen peaks, burning sands, sunken coasts, haunted abbeys and more, then an endless mode
- 168 hand-drawn monsters that move, snarl and attack
- Bosses with special rules that change how you play
- Weapons, armor, potions, wizard spells and cleric healing
- Level up, get knighted by the King, and name your own hero
- 3 save slots, 25 achievements and a Daily Challenge with the same cards for everyone
- Optional leaderboards and cloud saves

No ads. No in-app purchases.
