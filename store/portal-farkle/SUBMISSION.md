## Full description

```
Monster Farkle is real Farkle with a monster on the other side of the table.

Roll six dice. Set aside what scores and the blow you are winding up gets
heavier. Roll again and it gets heavier still. Strike, and everything you
have set aside lands on the monster at once.

Push one roll too far and you farkle: the whole wind-up is gone, your guard
drops, and the monster hits you harder for it. Knowing when to stop is the
whole game.

Ten worlds, forty areas, and a boss at the end of every one. Each boss
fights by a rule that changes the dice themselves, so the swing that carried
you through the last area will not carry you through this one. Between
fights there is a merchant: gear, charms and potions, and a bed if you can
pay for it.

Every monster is drawn by hand and most of them move. Your hero levels up,
hits harder, and keeps what they have earned even after a fall.

Scoring is traditional Farkle. Ones and fives score on their own, three of a
kind by face, four of a kind doubles it, and a run of six is the big one.
There is a scoring table in the menu, with real dice on it rather than a
wall of words.

No account, no sign-up. Open it and play.
```

## Controls

```
Tap or click a die to set it aside. Roll to push your luck. Strike to land
everything you have set aside. That is the whole game.
```

## Content rating notes

Say this plainly on the form rather than letting a reviewer find it:

- Cartoon fantasy violence. Monsters are knocked out, not killed, and there
  is no blood on a character.
- Skeletons, ghouls and other undead appear as drawn monsters.
- **Dice, but no gambling.** Nothing is wagered, bought or won with money,
  there is no simulated casino, and no currency leaves the game. The in-game
  gold buys gear from a merchant and exists only inside the run.

## What the game does not do

- No account, no sign-in, no personal data collected.
- No external links of any kind, in either package.
- No local storage beyond the player's own save, which stays on their
  device.
- No calls to any server except the portal's own SDK.
