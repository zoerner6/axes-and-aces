## CrazyGames

### Short description

```
A dark comic card adventure. Fight monsters with poker hands, rescue Anna, and
become Sir Edward.
```

### Long description

```
Bandits drag Anna down the cellar stairs of the Gilded Ace, and you are the only
soldier left standing. Axes & Aces is a dark comic adventure where every fight is
a hand of cards: the better the poker hand you play, the harder your hero swings.

A pair chips away at a goblin. A full house drops a troll. A straight flush takes
the head off a dragon.

Deal, discard, and pick your moment. Spend your gold on armor, weapons and
spellbooks between fights, learn which suits a boss shrugs off, and keep your
health up for the swing you can see coming. Ten quests, a hundred and sixty-eight
hand-drawn monsters that move and strike, and an Endless Abyss once the story is
done.

No sign-up. Nothing to buy. Just cards and monsters.
```

### Controls / instructions

```
Tap or click cards to pick the ones you want to play, then press Attack. The
hand you make decides the damage: the stronger the poker hand, the bigger the
hit. Press Discard to throw cards away and draw new ones. Press Hint if you
cannot see the best hand. Rest heals you but gives the monster a free swing.
Everything is mouse or touch; no keyboard needed.
```

## Poki

Poki is not an upload portal. You fill in a form at
`developers.poki.com/guide/share` and they decide whether to take the game.
The form asks for these.

### Describe your game in a few sentences

```
Axes & Aces is a dark comic card adventure. You play a soldier whose friend is
dragged off by bandits, and every fight is a hand of cards: the stronger the
poker hand you play, the harder you hit. Between fights you spend gold on armor,
weapons and spellbooks. Ten quests, 168 hand-drawn monsters that move and strike,
and an endless mode after the story. It is free, needs no sign-up, and plays on a
phone in portrait.
```

### Why do you think your game is a good fit for Poki?

```
It is a browser game first, built as one page of plain JavaScript with no engine,
so it starts in about a second and plays the same on a phone and a desktop. There
is no sign-up, nothing to buy, and no chat. A player understands it in one hand of
cards, and a run fits in a coffee break, but there are ten quests and an endless
mode behind it. Every monster is hand-drawn and animated.
```
